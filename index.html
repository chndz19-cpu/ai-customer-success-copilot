        How it works

        The workflow separates context capture, structured analysis, and action planning so the reasoning is repeatable and easy to review.

        01

        Capture

        Collect goals, lifecycle stage, adoption, sponsor engagement, recent interactions, and known blockers.

        02

        Analyze

        Evaluate account health signals, identify risks, and connect the customer’s stated goal to measurable success criteria.

        03

        Act

        Generate prioritized next actions, QBR talking points, and expansion signals without pushing growth before value is demonstrated.

        Why I built it

        I built this prototype to explore how AI-oriented workflows can help Customer Success teams move from reactive support to structured, proactive relationship management. It combines my background in client relationship management and portfolio growth with my ongoing development in AI, prompting, and data analytics.

        What I’m learning

        Prompt design • Structured workflows • Customer Success metrics • Responsible AI • Translating business context into repeatable processes

        Prototype note: This is a portfolio concept and functional front-end prototype.
